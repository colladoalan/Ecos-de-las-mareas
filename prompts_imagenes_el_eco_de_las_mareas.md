### (v4 — prompts en párrafos + personajes por escena; "escena" en vez de "plano")

Ficha de personajes actualizada con edades y rasgos exactos de las fichas visuales que compartiste:

- **Clara Martínez (42)** — madre y líder de la familia. Wavy chestnut-brown hair with lighter highlights, past shoulders, light blue-grey eyes, sun-kissed skin, no visible wrinkles. Wears a navy blue jacket over a grey shirt, dark trousers, boots. Calm, steady, authoritative presence. - **Tomás Martínez (45)** — padre y guía de la familia, 25+ años en el mar. Dark hair with light grey at the temples, short well-groomed beard, sun-kissed skin, deep brown eyes. Wears an olive-green utility vest over a grey/denim shirt. Protective, resilient, pragmatic presence, strong experienced hands.
- **Mateo Martínez (15)** — hijo mayor. Dark wavy tousled hair (slightly damp look), light hazel-green eyes, fair skin with light freckles, lean wiry teenage build. Plain navy t-shirt, black sports watch, small shell-pendant necklace. Reserved, observant, intense but restrained gaze; protective of his sister.
- **Sofía Martínez (9)** — hija menor. Light brown hair often in a loose braid, pale blue-grey eyes, fair skin. Wears a layered teal-pink-olive jacket and red sneakers, always carries her small blue plush octopus. Curious, sensitive, dreamy expression.
- **Alfi** — Sofía's palm-sized nanotech pet robot: smooth pale ceramic shell, glowing teal-blue seams and eyes, soft rounded shape, no visible wheels or legs (hovers/skitters).
- **Dr. Vicente Olivera** — Clara's late father, seen only in an aged photograph: elderly oceanographer, silver hair, weathered tan skin, khaki expedition gear, warm sepia tone, 2025-style photo.
- **El Benteveo (bote)** — modern planing-hull sailboat with a WHITE FIBERGLASS HULL (not wood-planked), 30ft/9.35m, a contemporary racing-cruiser design now 40 years old but lovingly, impeccably maintained — never neglected or run-down — NOT an antique wooden tall ship. Evenly sun-faded white gelcoat, clean and waxed, lovingly maintained, with one or two neat well-kept repairs, registration number "620772" painted near the bow, one or two neat, well-kept fixes on deck fittings, a single modern-size sailing wheel with a teak-veneer rim on a slim pedestal, in its correct normal orientation (compass console facing the helmsman, never mirrored), mounted in the open-air cockpit at the stern — no enclosed wheelhouse (twin underwater rudders, no second wheel), old mended tan-canela mainsail, composite deck with minor teak trim accents. Instrument panel mixes old analog dials with newer holographic displays retrofitted over the years — those are what die in the blockage.
